Maya'sCase File

10:47 PM. Tomorrow is Maya's big science test. Your team has been shrunk to the size of a cell and sent into her brain to help her study, but her brain just went into LOCKDOWN. Every room on the screen is sealed. The codes to open them are hidden in this Case File.

How to play

  1. Start the game on the laptop and choose With the printed Case File. You have 30 minutes.
  2. Click a room on the brain map. Its sealed door tells you which page of this Case File to solve.
  3. Read the room's field notes on the screen. Use them to solve the page with a pencil.
  4. Type the code into the door. Then beat the puzzle on screen to win one letter.
  5. Collect all 8 letters, then unscramble them in the frontal lobe to escape.

Start with the Brainstem. Nothing else opens until life support is back online. Split up to save time: the paper team can solve every page right away, while the screen team plays.

Memory tricks that work

Chunking: group numbers into pairs. Rehearsal: repeat them out loud. Teamwork: each person remembers one piece. Linking: connect a number to a picture ("the moon has 3 craters").

How neurons talk

An electrical pulse runs down the neuron → tiny bubbles called vesicles release neurotransmitters → they cross the gap (the synapse) → they fit into receptors on the next neuron, like a key in a lock.
