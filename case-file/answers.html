During the game

  • The clock is 30 minutes. Tell teams to split up: paper team solves pages, screen team plays rooms.
  • Brainstem room must be first on screen. Paper pages can be done in any order.
  • Every door and every puzzle has built-in hints on screen (each costs 30 seconds).
  • If a laptop breaks, the "Game Master guide" link on the title screen has Skip buttons.
  • Progress saves automatically, so refreshing the page is safe.

Afterward (5 min)

  1. Which room made your team use its frontal lobe most?
  2. What memory trick did your team use at the Memory Vault?
  3. When time got short, what did your amygdala do? What helped?
  4. Name one brain-healthy habit to use before a test.
